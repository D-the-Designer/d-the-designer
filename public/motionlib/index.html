MotionLib

Motion Reference Library — a library of short downloadable clips for motion reference: original performances by one gray stand-in figure, each rendered through a small set of camera treatments, plus public-domain film clips — expressions, scenes, chases — with their provenance shown. Download, paste, build. Free to use.
