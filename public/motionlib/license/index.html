What these are

Studies of timing, expression and energy, organised so a motion is easy to find and reuse. The originals are performed by a generic gray figure. Some are inspired by well-known moments in film history, the way a life-drawing pose might be — the motion is recreated, never traced from footage, and the figure depicts no real person.

What these are not

The original performances are not film footage and contain none. This library is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any studio, film, or performer. Any reference to an existing work is descriptive only. Public-domain clips are presented as what they are, with their source named on the page.

Using them with AI tools

These clips work as motion reference in tools that accept a reference video. What you generate with them, and whose likeness or characters you apply the motion to, is your responsibility and is covered by the terms of the tool you use.
