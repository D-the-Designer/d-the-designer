About

MotionLib — the Motion Reference Library — is a free library of short downloadable motion clips: expressions, gestures, reactions, scenes and chases. Download a clip, paste it into your project, done.

There are two shelves. Original performances: one simple gray stand-in figure — the same figure in every clip, never posed by the visitor, never changed — performing expressions and gestures, each rendered through a small fixed set of camera treatments. Think of it as Stand-In Studio's fixed sibling: there you pose the figure; here the performances are already made. Public-domain film clips: the great referenced moments of early cinema — cut tight, tagged by expression and action, each with its source film, year and public-domain basis on the page.

The main use is clean motion reference for video tools that take a reference clip, plus drawing reference and storyboarding. Everything is a static file: no accounts, no tracking, no server.

How the clips are made

Originals are animated once, then rendered through the library's camera treatments — a new treatment can be re-rendered across the whole catalogue without re-animating anything. Faces are separate movable pieces on the figure (brows, mouth), so an expression reads at any distance. Public-domain clips are cut from archival scans whose provenance is recorded on each page.
